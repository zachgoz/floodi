Restrict self-created profiles to unprivileged roles; privilege changes require trusted authority. This predates the forecast changes. Deployed rules were not inspected or changed here.
2. **Upgrade and revalidate dependencies.** Production-only npm audits report 25 flagged app packages (3 critical, 14 high, 8 moderate) and 22 Functions packages (3 critical, 8 high, 10 moderate, 1 low). The app manifest/lockfile are unchanged. An independent audit of the committed Functions lockfile returned the identical package/severity set: the new Google Auth dependency introduced no additional flagged package. Counts include dependency-chain findings and do not prove application exploitability. This review did not apply forced upgrades/downgrades.
3. **Run prospective event validation.** Score immutable issued-weather/model/input snapshots against later observations: peaks, onset, duration, missed floods and false alarms by lead/weather regime. Expand seasonal/event coverage. Realized-weather hindcasts and overlapping development windows remain limited evidence.
4. **Calibrate the physical reference and street depths.** Independently verify the existing 2.75 ft NAVD88-to-MLLW conversion, 60-minute tide localization and surveyed street elevations. The street metric remains a disclosed above-threshold proxy until calibrated.
5. **Add freshness alerts and archive retention.** Monitor overdue model/forecast jobs, missing gauge data and repeated provider/Vertex failures. Preserve prospective validation inputs while bounding private archive growth. Subsequently lazy-load map/analysis code to reduce the initial browser bundle.

The far historical baseline still fails the uniform 14-day skill comparison; issued-weather accuracy and reliable storm/event detection are not established. Those limitations remain in forecast metadata and detailed UI, without extending stale numerical forecasts or inventing measurements.
